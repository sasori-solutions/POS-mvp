Signature: quiet black controls, white background, editable content, concise Spanish text. Phone/tablet share the same functions and bottom-navigation destinations.
Typeface: IBM Plex Sans, regular and medium weights; roughly 16px controls, 20px primary data and compact headings. Pure white shell, near-black primary controls, light neutral supporting surfaces, gray secondary text.
Rhythm: touch controls at least 48px, generous vertical breathing room, content aligned left, no gradients or decoration. Authentication has one action per step: Google identity, business details, six-digit PIN, private business context.
Motion: no continuous animation; short input/button feedback. Respect reduced motion.
